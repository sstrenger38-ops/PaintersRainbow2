About this service

Keeping a property in good condition takes planning. Rainbow Painters offers renovation, repainting, repair coordination and ongoing maintenance — so owners, managers and associations have one dependable contractor for upkeep.

Benefits

  • One point of contactPainting, repairs and upkeep coordinated together.
  • Planned maintenancePeriodic repainting and touch-ups before problems grow.
  • Pre-sale & pre-rent refreshMake a property market-ready quickly.
  • Repair coordinationPlaster, putty, leakage and finishing issues handled together.
  • Flexible contractsOne-time renovation or recurring maintenance.
  • Clear communicationUpdates and sign-off at every stage.

Our work process

  1. Condition surveyReview the property and list the work needed.
  2. Plan & quotationPrioritised scope with an itemised estimate.
  3. Repair & renovationRepairs, repainting and finishing carried out in sequence.
  4. Maintenance scheduleOptional periodic visits to keep things in shape.

Suitable properties

HomeownersLandlords & rental propertiesApartment associationsCommercial landlordsInstitutionsPre-sale properties

Materials & finishing options

  • Interior and exterior paint systems
  • Plaster repair & putty
  • Waterproofing treatments
  • Wood and metal refinishing
  • Protective coatings

Final products and finishes are recommended after a site inspection, based on your surface and requirements.
