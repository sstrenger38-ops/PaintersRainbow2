About this service

Your home deserves a finish that looks beautiful and lasts. Rainbow Painters delivers complete house painting for independent houses, villas and apartments — from careful surface preparation to a clean, even final coat — led by a founder with 15+ years of hands-on experience.

Benefits

  • Proper surface preparationCracks, dampness and loose paint are treated before any coat is applied.
  • Clean, tidy executionFurniture, floors and fittings are protected and the site is cleaned up on completion.
  • Colour guidanceHelp choosing shades and finishes that suit your home and lighting.
  • Experienced painting teamsSkilled painters supervised on site for consistent quality.
  • Clear, itemised estimateYou know the scope and the quotation before work begins.
  • Planned timelinesWork is scheduled to keep disruption to your family minimal.

Our work process

  1. Free site inspectionWe visit, measure and understand your requirements.
  2. Surface preparationScraping, crack filling, putty and primer where needed.
  3. PaintingPrimer and finish coats applied by experienced painters.
  4. Touch-up & handoverFinal inspection with you, then a thorough clean-up.

Suitable properties

Independent housesVillasApartments & flatsGated-community homesRental propertiesPre-sale repainting

Materials & finishing options

  • Interior emulsions — matt, soft-sheen and silk finishes
  • Weather-resistant exterior emulsions
  • Textured and decorative wall finishes
  • Primers, wall putty and crack-filling compounds
  • Enamel for doors, grills and trims

Final products and finishes are recommended after a site inspection, based on your surface and requirements.

FAQ

House Painting — Common Questions

How long does a house repaint take?

It depends on size, condition and scope. After the site inspection we give you a realistic schedule along with the estimate.

Do I need to move out during painting?

Usually not. We work room by room and protect your belongings, but we will advise based on your property.

Can you help me choose colours?

Yes — we can suggest shade combinations and finishes suitable for your rooms and exteriors.
