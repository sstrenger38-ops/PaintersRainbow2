🏭 Industrial Painting

Heavy-duty painting and protective coating solutions for industrial buildings, factories and facilities.

About this service

Industrial environments demand coatings that protect as well as finish. Rainbow Painters undertakes heavy-duty painting for factories, sheds, warehouses and plant facilities, with correct surface preparation and systems chosen for the conditions on site.

Benefits

  • Protection, not just colourCoating systems selected to resist corrosion, moisture and wear.
  • Correct surface preparationRust removal and priming before protective coats.
  • Safety-conscious teamsWork planned with site safety rules and access requirements.
  • Large-scale capabilityRoof sheets, structural steel, walls, floors and pipelines.
  • Minimal downtimePhased execution around production schedules.
  • Contract flexibilityOne-time projects or recurring maintenance painting.

Our work process

  1. Technical inspectionAssess substrate, environment and coating requirements.
  2. Specification & quoteRecommended coating system with a detailed quotation.
  3. Preparation & coatingCleaning, rust treatment, primer and protective top coats.
  4. Quality checkInspection and handover with a clear completion record.

Suitable properties

Factories & plantsWarehouses & shedsSteel structuresMachinery shopsIndustrial floorsPipelines & tanks

Materials & finishing options

  • Anti-corrosive primers and epoxy systems
  • Polyurethane (PU) top coats
  • Heavy-duty industrial enamels
  • Epoxy floor coatings
  • Heat-resistant and special-purpose coatings (on request)

Final products and finishes are recommended after a site inspection, based on your surface and requirements.
