About this service

Builders, developers and facility managers often need dependable painting manpower. Rainbow Painters provides experienced painting teams and contract-based workforce services for residential, commercial and industrial projects — supervised by a contractor with 15+ years in the trade.

Benefits

  • Experienced paintersSkilled hands for interior, exterior and finishing work.
  • Supervised teamsOn-site supervision for consistent quality.
  • Flexible engagementLabour-only or complete labour-and-material contracts.
  • Scalable manpowerTeam size matched to your schedule.
  • Reliable coordinationSingle point of contact for your site team.
  • Fair, transparent termsRates and scope agreed clearly in advance.

Our work process

  1. Requirement discussionScope, duration, team size and site conditions.
  2. ProposalRates and terms for labour-only or full contract.
  3. MobilisationTeam deployed with supervision on site.
  4. Review & close-outProgress reviews and completion sign-off.
