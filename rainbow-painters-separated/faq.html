Frequently Asked Questions

How much does house painting cost?

Cost depends on the area, surface condition, paint system and scope. We provide a clear, itemised estimate after understanding your requirement — request one online or book a free site inspection.

Do you provide free site inspection?

Yes. Rainbow Painters offers a free site inspection so we can understand your property and prepare an accurate quotation.

Do you handle both interior and exterior painting?

Yes. We handle interior, exterior or both, with proper surface preparation and finishing.

Do you provide commercial painting contracts?

Yes. We undertake offices, shops, hotels, apartments and institutional buildings, with schedules planned around your operations.

Do you provide industrial painting?

Yes. We provide heavy-duty painting and protective coatings for factories, warehouses and industrial facilities.

Do you provide waterproofing?

Yes. We treat roofs, terraces, walls, bathrooms, basements and other leakage-prone areas, and can combine it with repainting.

Can I request an online estimate?

Yes. Fill in the Get Your Painting Estimate form with your property details, or send the details on WhatsApp.

Do you provide labour-only contracts?

Yes. We provide skilled painting teams on a labour-only or labour-and-material basis for residential, commercial and industrial projects.

Which locations do you serve?

We serve Chennai and Andhra Pradesh. Please contact us to confirm service availability in your area.
