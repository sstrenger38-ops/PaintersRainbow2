Professional finishing for doors, windows, furniture, grills, gates and metal structures.

About this service

Doors, windows, gates and grills take the most wear. Rainbow Painters restores and finishes wood and metal with the right preparation — rust treatment, sanding and primers — followed by polish, enamel or PU finishes that look good and protect.

Benefits

  • Rust and wear treatedOld paint, rust and damage addressed before finishing.
  • Refined wood finishesPolish, melamine and PU for a rich, protective surface.
  • Durable metal coatingsAnti-rust primers and weather-resistant enamels.
  • Sharp detailingClean edges on grills, mouldings and ornamental work.
  • Indoor & outdoor workFinishes selected for sun and rain exposure where needed.
  • Quick turnaroundEfficient scheduling for gates, doors and fixtures.

Our work process

  1. AssessmentCheck surface condition and desired finish.
  2. Stripping & preparationRemove old finish, treat rust, sand and prime.
  3. FinishingPolish, enamel or PU applied in multiple coats.
  4. InspectionFinal check for smoothness, colour and coverage.

Suitable properties

Main doors & interior doorsWindows & framesWooden furnitureGrills & railingsGates & shuttersMetal structures

Materials & finishing options

  • Wood polish and melamine finishes
  • PU wood coatings
  • Anti-rust primers
  • Synthetic and PU enamels
  • Clear protective lacquers

Final products and finishes are recommended after a site inspection, based on your surface and requirements.
