About this service

Whether you are refreshing a single room or repainting an entire property, Rainbow Painters delivers complete interior and exterior painting with the preparation that makes a finish last — smooth walls inside, weather-ready protection outside.

Benefits

Of course. You can choose interior, exterior or both — the estimate form lets you select.

Will you help with damp patches?

We assess the cause first. Where waterproofing is needed we can recommend and carry it out.

How do you protect my furniture?
